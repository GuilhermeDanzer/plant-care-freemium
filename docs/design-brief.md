# PlantHelper — Design Brief

## One-liner

PlantHelper is an offline-first plant-care companion that turns a photo into a
care plan — identify any plant with AI, diagnose its health, and get a watering
reminder exactly when it's needed, even with no internet connection.

## Mood

**Calm, reassuring, grounded.** The app should feel like a knowledgeable,
patient gardening friend — never anxious, never noisy. A quiet confidence that
"your plants are in good hands." Every screen should lower the user's
heart-rate, not raise it.

## Style

**Minimal and botanical.** Generous whitespace, soft rounded cards, understated
typography, and one clear action per screen. Leaf imagery used sparingly as
accents rather than decoration. Nothing gimmicky — everything feels intentional
and effortless.

## Colors

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Forest Green | `#1F4D3A` | brand, primary buttons, active states |
| Anchor | Dark Navy | `#0E1B2C` | headings, nav, dark surfaces |
| Background | Warm Off-White | `#FAF9F5` | base canvas, cards |
| Secondary | Soft Sage | `#8FB79B` | secondary surfaces, highlights, progress |
| Accent | Muted Gold | `#D4A24E` | streaks, achievements, premium |
| Semantic · Healthy | Emerald | `#10B981` | health state: perfect |
| Semantic · Needs care | Amber | `#D97706` | health state: optimization |
| Semantic · Urgent | Rose | `#E11D48` | health state: critical |

## Typography direction

Single sans-serif family, generous line-height. Strong size contrast between
screen titles and body text. Numbers (streaks, dates, frequency) set in a
tabular/monospace cut so they align cleanly in lists and reminders.

## Motion

Soft and slow — subtle fade/scale-in transitions, gentle haptic feedback on
watering and completing a streak. No bouncy or frantic animations; motion should
feel like growth, not noise.
