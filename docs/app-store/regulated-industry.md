# Regulated industry & protected third-party material

## Regulated industry

**Not applicable.** PlantHelper is a consumer lifestyle/gardening app. It does
not operate in a highly regulated industry (no health/medical claims, no
financial services, no gambling, no alcohol/tobacco, no legal/insurance
services).

## Protected third-party material

The app does **not** reproduce protected third-party material such as music,
film, books, news, or sports content.

- **Botanical knowledge base:** plant descriptions, taxonomy, care tips and
  images are provided under license by the **Kindwise / Plant.id API**. The
  lexicon detail screens surface source citations and license metadata where
  the API returns them (`citation`, `license_name`, `license_url` fields in
  `src/types/lexicon.ts`).
- **User-generated content:** photos users upload are their own; there is no
  public feed, commenting, or sharing of other users' content (a milestone
  "share" only exports an image of the user's own plant to the system share
  sheet).

**Documentation/credentials required:** none. No additional authorization
documents are needed for this app.
