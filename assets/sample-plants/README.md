# Sample plant images (for the App Review screen recording)

Drop two real photos here so the recording shows the AI identification and
health-assessment flows convincingly. Suggested (public-domain) sources:

- `healthy-monstera.jpg` — a healthy Monstera leaf, e.g. from Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Monstera_deliciosa_(leaves)
- `diseased-leaf.jpg` — a leaf with visible damage/spots for the health scan:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Plant_diseases

If you don't have images, use your own phone photos of a houseplant and a
leaf with blemishes. The filenames are referenced in
`docs/app-store/app-review-demo-script.md`.

These images are **not** committed if you prefer to keep them private — they
are only used locally while recording.
